Semiconductor device physics

Build or refresh the foundations: band structure, electrostatics, carrier transport, junctions, and how physical assumptions shape device behavior.

Avalanche & single-photon detectors

Explore multiplication, breakdown, noise, detection probability, and modeling considerations in APDs and SPADs.

03

III-nitride & wide-bandgap devices

Connect polarization, heterostructures, high-field transport, and material properties to electronic and photonic performance.

Modeling with physical judgment

Evaluate model assumptions, numerical methods, calibration, and the limits of simulation as an engineering tool.

05

Semiconductor photonics
