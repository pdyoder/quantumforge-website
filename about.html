About

QuantumForge Semiconductor Modeling, LLC is an independent consulting practice focused on advanced modeling and simulation solutions for the semiconductor industry.

With deep expertise in device physics, numerical methods, and TCAD, QuantumForge helps clients accelerate innovation, reduce technical risk, and optimize device performance.

The practice works with startups, established companies, and research organizations to solve complex challenges and bring better devices to market.

Areas of Expertise

  • Power devices and wide-bandgap technologies
  • Optoelectronic and photonic semiconductor devices
  • Advanced CMOS, FinFET, and GAA devices
  • Device/circuit/system co-optimization
  • Compact model development and calibration
  • Algorithm development and custom simulation solutions
